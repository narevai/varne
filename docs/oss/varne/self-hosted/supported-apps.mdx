## What if my application is not here

Varne still lists it, with its name taken from the image, its cost, and a logo when it has one. It has no subscription to compare with, so it adds to the cost but not to the savings.

The catalog lives in `src/varne/catalog/apps.py` in the [Varne repository](https://github.com/narevai/varne). To add an application, add an entry with the names its images go by, the subscription it replaces, and the price of that subscription's most popular plan, and open a pull request. Check the price on the provider's own pricing page, in USD.
